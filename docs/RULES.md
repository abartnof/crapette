# Rules of Crapette (Russian Bank / Zank-Patience)

Crapette is a competitive two-player patience (solitaire) card game played with two standard 52-card decks (104 cards total), preferably with distinct backs (e.g. Red for Player 1, Blue for Player 2 / Opponent).

## 1. Setup & Layout
1. **The Reserve ("Crapette" / Depot)**:
   - Each player deals 13 cards from their pack into a face-down pile, turning the 13th (top) card face-up.
   - Symmetrically placed on the player's right.
2. **The Tableau (Houses)**:
   - 8 houses total arranged between the players.
   - Initialized by dealing 4 face-up cards per player in front of their reserve.
   - Shared territory: either player may build on any of the 8 houses.
3. **The Central Foundations**:
   - 8 center foundation spaces (2 for each suit: ♠, ♥, ♦, ♣).
   - Started exclusively by Aces.
   - Built upward in ascending order by suit ($A \to 2 \to 3 \to \dots \to K$).
   - Communal: both players contribute to all 8 foundations.
4. **The Hand (Stock / Talon)**:
   - The remaining 35 cards ($52 - 13 - 4 = 35$) held face down to the player's left.
5. **The Waste Pile (Discard / Écart)**:
   - Starts empty to the right of the Stock.
   - Unplayable cards drawn from the stock are placed here, ending the turn.

## 2. Order of Play
- **First Move**: The player whose initial face-up Reserve card has the lower rank plays first ($A=1$ low, $K=13$ high). Ties broken by comparing tableau cards.
- **Turn Sequence**:
  - The player may make any number of legal moves in succession from their available cards.
  - When finished with tableau/reserve moves, the player flips the top card of their Hand (Stock).
  - If playable, the player may play it and continue moving cards or turning more Stock cards.
  - If unplayable, the card is placed on the Waste pile, which immediately ends the turn.
- **Stock Exhaustion**:
  - When the Stock is empty and a card must be drawn, the Waste pile is flipped over *without shuffling* to form a new Stock.

## 3. Building Rules
- **Foundations**: Ascending sequence in the *same suit* ($A \to 2 \to \dots \to K$). Once placed, cards never return to the tableau.
- **Tableau (Houses)**: Descending sequence with *strictly alternating colors* (e.g. Red 9 on Black 10). Single card moves standard; packed sequences can move if spaces permit.
- **Empty Tableau Spaces**: Can be filled by any available card.
- **Opponent Loading ("Crapette" / Dumping)**:
  - An available card may be placed onto the opponent's exposed Reserve top card or opponent's exposed Waste pile top card.
  - **Restrictions**: Must be the *exact same suit* and *adjacent rank* (+1 or -1, e.g. on 8♠ you may place 7♠ or 9♠). Aces and Kings do not wrap.

## 4. Compulsory Moves & "Crapette!" Penalty
Under standard competitive rules:
1. **Priority 1 (Reserve to Foundation)**: If the player's Reserve top card can go to a Foundation, that move *must* be made before any other.
2. **Priority 2 (General Foundation)**: Any available card that can go to a Foundation must be played before drawing from Stock.
3. **Priority 3 (Empty Space from Reserve)**: A player cannot draw from Stock while an empty tableau space exists if their Reserve still has cards.
4. **The "Crapette!" Call**:
   - If the active player commits an oversight or illegal move, the opponent calls **"Crapette!"** (or "Stop!").
   - The attempted move is retracted, the compulsory move is completed, and the active player's turn immediately forfeits to the opponent!

## 5. Winning & Tournament Scoring
- The deal ends the instant a player plays their final card (emptying Reserve, Stock, and Waste).
- **Match Points**:
  - Winner receives a **30-point** victory bonus.
  - Winner scores **2 points** for each card remaining in the loser's Reserve.
  - Winner scores **1 point** for each card remaining in the loser's Stock and Waste.
